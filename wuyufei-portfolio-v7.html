CASE 01 / USER RESEARCH

预售体验研究Pre-sale experience research

从 3,000+ 条商家原声，拆解发货规则与经营感受之间的关系。Mapped the relationship between shipping rules and merchant sentiment from 3,000+ voice records.

51.4%

问题定义：Problem: 预售发货慢为何影响用户取消与商家经营感受？Why does slow pre-sale shipping affect cancellations and merchant sentiment?

研究过程：Process: 归类 3,000+ 条商家原声；对 C 端取消行为与 B 端反馈进行双视角整理；完成竞品规则对比。Categorized 3,000+ merchant voice records, structured both user and merchant perspectives, and compared competitor rules.

结论：Outcome: 51.4% 用户因发货时间长取消预售订单，46.4% 腰部商家认为物流规则影响经营。51.4% of users cancelled due to long shipping times, and 46.4% of mid-tier merchants felt logistics rules affected operations.

CASE 02 / MARKET INSIGHT

户外夹克行业分析Outdoor jacket analysis

比较品类增长、品牌份额与消费者需求，提炼轻量化 + 多场景逻辑。Compared category growth, brand share and consumer needs to identify a lightweight, multi-scenario opportunity.

+24%

问题定义：Problem: 户外夹克增长来自哪些品类与消费需求变化？Which categories and consumer needs are driving outdoor jacket growth?

研究过程：Process: 拆解 15+ 竞品，比较品类增速、品牌份额与产品卖点。Compared 15+ competitors across category growth, brand share and product propositions.

结论：Outcome: 非三合一品类增长 +24%，三合一 -1%，提炼“轻量化 + 多场景”的增长逻辑。Non-3-in-1 grew 24% while 3-in-1 declined 1%, pointing to a lightweight, multi-scenario opportunity.

CASE 03 / DATA ANALYSIS

电商数据分析E-commerce data analysis

用 R / tidyverse 处理十万级行为数据，发现 Q4 假期前 7 天购买意愿提升。Used R / tidyverse on 100k-scale behavior data and identified a pre-holiday purchase window.

+40%

问题定义：Problem: 如何从十万级用户行为数据中识别购买时机？How can purchase timing be identified from 100k-scale behavior data?

研究过程：Process: 使用 filter、na.omit、inner_join 完成清洗与整合，用 ggplot2 绘制留存曲线。Used filter, na.omit and inner_join for cleaning and joins, then plotted retention curves with ggplot2.

结论：Outcome: 数据准确率提升至 98%，发现 Q4 假期前 7 天购买意愿提升 40%。Improved accuracy to 98% and found a 40% increase in purchase intent during the seven days before the Q4 holiday.
